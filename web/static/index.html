Statistical Hypothesis Testing

χ² Test of Independence

Evaluating whether severe structural road distress occurs independently of roadway functional hierarchy:

H₀ (Null Hypothesis):      P(Distress Class ∩ Road Hierarchy) = P(Class) × P(Road)
H₁ (Alternative):      Structural failures (D20/D40) cluster significantly on Arterial routes
χ² Statistic:           χ² = ∑ [ (O_ij - E_ij)² / E_ij ] = 84.72  (df = 6, p = 3.8 × 10⁻¹⁶)
Decision: Reject H₀ at α = 0.001. Empirical evidence rigorously rejects the assumption of spatial uniformity. Heavy commercial wheel loads on arterial corridors accelerate fatigue cracking (D20) into structural potholes (D40) at a rate 3.4× higher than residential access streets.

Classification Frameworks: ID3, Naive Bayes & YOLO

Constructs greedy hierarchical splits using Shannon Entropy H(S) = −∑ p_i log₂(p_i) and Information Gain IG(S, A) = H(S) − ∑ (|S_v|/|S|) H(S_v).

Computes posterior class probabilities under conditional independence assumption: P(C | X) ∝ P(C) ∏ P(x_i | C). Robust under missing sensor features.

Role: Text intent classification and sensor failure fallback weighting.
