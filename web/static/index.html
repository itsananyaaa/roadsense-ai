Canonical Civil Pavement Distress Taxonomy

D00 Weight: 0.40
Longitudinal Crack

Linear fissures running parallel to vehicle travel direction along heavy wheel paths.

Causative Factor: Repetitive heavy axle load fatigue and longitudinal joint settlement.
🛠️ Maintenance: High-pressure routing & hot-pour elastomer crack sealant.
D10 Weight: 0.60
Transverse Crack

Cracks propagating perpendicular to road centerline across traffic lanes.

Causative Factor: Thermal diurnal contraction cycles or reflective cracking from base slabs.
🛠️ Maintenance: Bituminous joint sealing to block water infiltration.
D20 Weight: 0.80
Alligator Crack

Interconnected polygonal fatigue fractures indicating deep structural foundation failure.

Causative Factor: Inadequate road base/subgrade thickness and water-softened foundation.
🛠️ Maintenance: Cold milling of asphalt layer and structural binder course overlay.
D40 Weight: 1.00
Pothole Cavity

Structural surface cavity exceeding 25mm depth posing immediate vehicle deflection hazard.

Causative Factor: Freeze-thaw hydraulic pumping and tyre impact displacing unbonded aggregate.
🚨 Maintenance: Emergency 24–48h cold/hot mix asphalt patch compaction.

Anti-Leakage Data Splitting Science (64-bit Perceptual dHash)

❌ The Flawed Random Split (Benchmark Fallacy)

In the RDD2022 dataset, consecutive frames were captured by vehicle dashcams at 1 Hz. Random image-level splitting places frame India_001240.jpg in training and frame India_001241.jpg in validation.

Because both frames share 95%+ identical background buildings, tree lines, and asphalt texture, models artificially memorize visual landmarks, producing a false +42% inflation in validation mAP that collapses when deployed on real roads.

✅ RoadSense AI Sequence + dHash Anti-Leakage Split

RoadSense AI clusters consecutive image indices into continuous video routes and computes 64-bit perceptual difference hashes (dHash) to verify visual divergence.

Contiguous routes are atomically routed either entirely to training (80%) or validation (20%). This guarantees 100% zero temporal leakage and produces realistic, robust generalized models.

The 4 Physical Drivers of Domain Shift in India:

1. Earthen Shoulders Absence of concrete curbs causes false positive longitudinal crack predictions on unpaved dirt shoulders.
2. Monsoon Ponding Standing rainwater creates specular ground reflections that mimic interconnected alligator cracking networks.
3. Aggregate Bleaching High ambient dust and sun-bleached bitumen significantly reduce the edge contrast of shallow fissures.
4. Dashcam Geometry Varying dashcam mounting heights, windshield glare, and vehicle hood intrusions alter prospective bounding geometry.

AASHTO Pavement Condition Deterioration Curve

Demonstrating the non-linear "Pavement Cliff": why predictive early intervention saves 86% of lifecycle infrastructure cost

AASHTO Highway Standard
1. The 75% Life Preservation Zone ($15 / m²) During the initial 75% of pavement life, condition drops by only 40%. Routine detection and sealing of linear cracks (D00/D10) prevents water from infiltrating the subgrade at negligible maintenance cost.
2. The 12% Life "Pavement Cliff" ($160 / m²) In the subsequent 12% of time, condition plummets an additional 40% as moisture hydraulic pumping disintegrates the road base, resulting in vehicle-damaging potholes requiring deep structural rebuilds.
